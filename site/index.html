Why Sasayaki

It is just the app. You pick the servers, and nothing else gets your voice.

Works in every app
The bubble shows up when your keyboard opens, in chats, mail, notes and browsers. You can also start it from your keyboard's mic key.
Cleans up what you say
Filler words go, and “8, no wait, 9” becomes “9”. Each app can get its own style, casual for chats and full sentences for mail.
Use any OpenAI-compatible server
Run whisper.cpp, faster-whisper or llama.cpp at home, or use a hosted service. Cleanup is optional.
Private
No cloud backup, and audio only goes to the servers you set up. It won't record in password or banking fields.
Gets names right
Add names and jargon once, and both the speech and the cleanup model spell them your way.
Small, with no bloat
About 4 MB. No ads, no trackers, no account and nothing running except the bubble.

Get Sasayaki

Install with Obtainium for automatic updates, or download the APK. You also need a speech-to-text server. The setup guide lists some options.
